# S2 structured interview (run by the master with AskUserQuestion)

Why structured: free-form "interview me" asks generic questions and surfaces under half of implicit
requirements, and almost none about look and feel. A checklist of aspects lifts coverage about a third.

## Aspects to cover (track which are done)
1. Persona & situation: who exactly, in what moment
2. Trigger: what happens right before they need this
3. Input: what they bring (file, text, link, data)
4. Output: what they need to walk away with
5. Done: how they know it worked
6. Today: what they use now and what annoys them (link to W2 weakness)
7. Constraints: data, privacy, platform, time
8. **Look & feel**: always ask explicitly (tone, density, reference app, screenshot)

## Rules
- Up to 4 questions per AskUserQuestion call, at most 5 calls (20 questions).
- Each question offers 2–4 concrete options drawn from the evidence (quotes, competitor features),
  first option marked (Recommended) when the evidence clearly favours it. The user can always pick Other.
- Prefer questions about past behaviour and real examples over hypotheticals.
- Stop early when all 8 aspects are covered.
- Output: 3 candidate core-job sentences (<20 words, "<persona> can <job> without <weakness>"),
  then one AskUserQuestion to pick, and a list of aspects still unclear.
